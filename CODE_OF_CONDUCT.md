# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Appropriate action will be taken based on the circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will review reported incidents fairly and take appropriate action to maintain a respectful environment.
